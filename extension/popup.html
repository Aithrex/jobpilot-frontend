Checking…
Aspirely fills standard fields (name, contact, links) and drafts résumé-grounded answers you can edit — only into empty fields, never overwriting your text. It never submits for you: every submit is your own click, unless you approve an application for auto-submit and turn on the switch below.
Auto-fill when a form is detected
Fills applications the moment you open them — no clicking needed. Only empty fields; sensitive questions stay yours.
Auto-continue steps
On multi-step forms (e.g. Workday), clicks the site's Next/Continue once a step is complete — after a countdown you can cancel. Never clicks Submit.
Allow auto-submit on this device
Off = review-only (default). When on, Aspirely may auto-submit only applications you approved in the app — after a countdown you can cancel. Never touches CAPTCHAs.
Advanced · connect manually
Signing in to the app auto-syncs your token. If that isn't working, paste the JWT here.
