Sensitive fields still pause for you.

Salary expectations, sponsorship, disability and veteran status, notice period, and anything a posting marks as a required free text answer. Auto Apply stops, holds the application, and asks. It does not guess on your behalf, and it does not leave a field blank to get past a form.

06  ·  guardrails

What it will never do.

Invent an answer

If the form asks something your resume does not answer, it pauses. There is no default, no best guess, no filler.

Apply twice

One application per req, per company, per quarter. Reposts of the same role are recognized and skipped.

Send an untailored file

If tailoring failed or the diff was not approved, the application stays queued rather than going out generic.

Hide what it sent

Every send writes a receipt: which file, which fields, which timestamp. Exportable, and yours.
