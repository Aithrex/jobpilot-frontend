Paper and ink. One confident accent.

Hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from color. Indigo is reserved for the one thing you should do next and for a match that is worth your attention. Ochre only ever means recency or a pause that needs you.

04  ·  motion

Reveal, count, settle. Nothing bounces.

easing cubic-bezier(.22,.72,.16,1)
reveal 640ms, 16px rise, clip-path mask
stagger 40ms, capped at 12 items
count up 1100ms, cubic ease out
ring draw 1400ms, follows the count
theme change 400ms on background and color
reduced motion everything resolves instantly

Forbidden: parallax, bounce or spring easing, scale on hover above 1.02, anything that moves while the user is reading. The scroll locked ribbon on the landing page is the only long scroll effect, and it advances one step at a time rather than scrubbing.

06  ·  house rules

What this system does and refuses.

Always

  • Hierarchy from size and weight before color.
  • Numbered kickers on every major section.
  • Mono for anything the system reports back.
  • Hairline dividers instead of cards where possible.
  • A receipt under every generated surface.
  • Left aligned. The page has a spine.
  • Plain copy. No em dashes, no accented characters.

Never

  • Gradient blobs, glassmorphism, neon glow.
  • Drop shadows on product surfaces.
  • Donut charts or any dashboard cliche.
  • Center stacked hero with three feature cards.
  • Stock photography or sparkle iconography.
  • Hype verbs: revolutionize, unleash, supercharge.
  • A logo on the proof strip without a real interview behind it.
